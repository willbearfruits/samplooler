A session, 40 seconds, at the board's 30 frames a second

  • 0:00 the boot
  • 0:04 REC held: it listens, and the take starts at the first sound
  • 0:07 the loop, tilted faster, backwards, with delay, stutter, grains

  • 0:25 the left knob drags the tape

  • 0:33 flung at 8x, then the crawl

What changed in the Samplooler's firmware (0.2)

  • One image for both boards. At power-on it looks for a joystick; without one it takes the left knob. Rev C plays as before.
  • The left knob moves the tape (JOG). Its push is REC. PLAY > Knob makes it SPEED, PITCH or FILTER.
  • A hand over the sensor closes a filter. PLAY > Hand makes it PITCH, BRAKE, VOLUME, ECHO or OFF.
  • A take starts at the first sound and leaves REC's click out. PLAY > Loop start: SOUND or PRESS.
  • The screen has its own task. It sends only what changed, up to 30 frames a second, and no longer holds up the controls.
  • The menu is a list in groups, with the value edited in place.
  • SYSTEM > Board check shows every switch, knob and sensor as it is read.
  • A bug fixed: a loop loaded from a slot next to other boards started off the beat half the time.

The other programs on rev E

The same two parts in the other programs: the left knob (its push is REC) and a hand over the sensor, from 25 cm down to 4 cm. Each clip is the program's own screen with the sound it made in its simulator. The clips start muted: unmute one to hear it. Every range and timing is a first guess, unheard on a board.

HUFF

Left knobthe octave, two up and two down (PLAY > OCTAVE)
Handa mute: the lower the hand the darker; waved, it is a wah. PITCH > HAND DOES: MUTE, VIBRATO, BEND UP, BEND DOWN

0:01 octaves up and down · 0:05 the hand comes down, sits, lifts · 0:09 waved

Open: a face close to the mic may read as a hand. Every screen

WRECK

Left knobcrush to the left, drive to the right, 12 detents to full
Handthe roll, faster the lower the hand. WRECK > HAND: ROLL, DIVE, CRUSH, DRIVE

0:01 full crush · 0:03 full drive · 0:05 back to the middle · 0:06 the hand comes down: the roll

Open: if the knob counts the other way, crush and drive swap. Every screen

MANTRA

Left knobthe note, a semitone a detent, an octave either way (HANDS > NOTE)
Handa hand over his mouth: it closes to a hum. HANDS > HAND: HUM, VIBRATO

0:01 the knob walks a tune and comes home · 0:05 the hand comes down: AH closes to a hum · 0:08 it lifts

Every screen

BAND

Left knobthe live layer's level, 5 % a detent (the mixer's own value)
Handone move for the live layer: DRUMS roll, BASS filter opens, LEAD bends down, KEYS more motion, BREATH vibrato, HOWL more gain

0:01 KEYS turned down, then up · 0:05 the hand on KEYS: motion · 0:10 the hand on DRUMS: a roll

Lost against the stick: drum repeat and filter, breath bend, bass accent. Every screen

PIXEL

Left knobin the editor it moves the cursor while the right knob writes; with the right knob held it changes the row. In play it is the tempo, 1 BPM a detent
Handthe breakdown: LEAD out below 19 cm, CHORDS below 14, BASS below 9. The drums stay

First clip: a bar written with the two knobs, then the left knob pushed at 0:07 to hear it.

Second clip: 0:01 lead out · 0:01.5 chords · 0:02 bass · 0:03 to 0:04 they come back as the hand lifts.

Lost against the stick: muting one part alone, or the drums. Every screen
