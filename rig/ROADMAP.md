# Roadmap: after the first build

- **New gestures, chosen from recorded takes, not guessed:**
  - scratch with the yaw rate (a turntable);
  - a flick to reverse;
  - a tap to restart the loop;
  - shake to scatter.

  The rig's replay bench scores detectors on takes before anything goes into the firmware.
- **Automatic tuning:** sweep the tuning values over a set of takes and keep the best by the metrics.
- **A virtual ALSA MIDI port,** so a DAW and the plugin can share the board while the rig runs.
- **The core instruments on the rig:** they already stream `m`, and `q`, `O` and `T` would come next.
- **Two boards on the flock** in one view: beat, tempo, who is there.
- **The rev D model** (one board, the OLED under it).
- **Flashing from the viewer,** with the user's confirmation and never the TWOFOLD.
- **A recorded session played back** in the viewer as a video, for social posts.
