Gesture recorder pin map

Hold BOOT to record the mic, tilt to play it back forwards or backwards. Both maps are what the firmware uses. The C3 map fits the plain SuperMini and the variant with the 0.42" screen on board.

Budget. No PSRAM, so the take lives in SRAM: the firmware measures what is free at boot and takes it, about 5 to 7 s at 16 kHz in 16-bit. No FPU, so the audio path is Q15 integer math.
